About Akkoro Pacific

Who we are, what we stand for, and why the Pacific.

Our Mission

Securing the Mission. Powering the Pacific.

Akkoro Pacific LLC exists to deliver enterprise-grade technology solutions to the organizations that keep the U.S. Pacific region secure, operational, and connected. We partner with federal agencies, DoD components, territorial governments, and private enterprises to modernize infrastructure, harden cybersecurity postures, and integrate AI-driven capabilities — where and when it matters most.

The Pacific is not an afterthought. It is a strategic theater, home to critical U.S. military installations, territorial governments, and a rapidly evolving digital landscape. Akkoro Pacific is built to understand this environment and operate within it at the highest levels of sensitivity and trust.

Our Vision

The Trusted Technology Partner of the Pacific.

We envision a Pacific region where government agencies and defense organizations operate with the same technological capability as their mainland counterparts — with solutions tailored to the geographic, operational, and compliance realities of the Pacific AOR.

Akkoro Pacific aspires to hold positions on the major defense and civilian IT contract vehicles serving the Pacific, building a reputation for delivery, integrity, and cleared capability.

Our Focus Area

Why the Pacific?

The name Akkoro Pacific reflects a deliberate strategic choice. The U.S. Pacific region — spanning Hawaii, Guam, the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands, American Samoa, and Pacific CONUS — is an area of immense national security importance and, historically, underserved technology capacity.

We bring mainland-caliber technology expertise to Pacific-region clients, with the regional knowledge and presence needed to operate effectively in these unique environments.
